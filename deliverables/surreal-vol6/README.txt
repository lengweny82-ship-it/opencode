TECHNICAL NOTES
Every final PNG is 7000 x 7000 pixels, 8-bit RGBA, with actual transparency outside the artwork and in identified internal background gaps. Warm ivory details, painted highlights and colored decorative forms remain intentionally opaque.
The source artwork was generated at 1024 x 1024 and enlarged with Lanczos interpolation, not native 7000px detail or AI super-resolution.
RGB colors use 32 levels per channel for file-size control; alpha remains 8-bit.
Image 01: a thin hanging chain that ran into the original canvas boundary was removed above the sun ornament; the sun, elephant and garden remain.
Each PNG has been checked for dimensions, RGBA format and transparent exterior. Source cutouts were reviewed on dark, light and magenta backgrounds.
manifest.json lists final PNG sizes and SHA-256 hashes; transparency-checks.json records the interior background removal checks.
Always inspect fine details and the product mockup at your chosen print size before publishing. Screen HEX colors are visual references, not guaranteed fabric colors; select the closest available garment shade.
