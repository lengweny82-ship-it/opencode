TECHNICAL NOTES
Every final PNG is 7000 x 7000 pixels, 8-bit RGBA, with actual exterior transparency. Identified internal background gaps are transparent in the character cutouts. Eyes, teeth, pale clothing, highlights and plates remain intentionally opaque. Cypher's circular badge has an intentional opaque interior; only its outside is transparent.
All source images were generated at 1408 x 768 pixels. The illustrations were cropped, fitted without stretching into square transparent canvases, and enlarged using Lanczos interpolation. Cypher is an intentionally cropped circular portrait rather than a full-body design. These are not native 7000px-detail images or AI super-resolution.
RGB colors use 32 levels per channel for file-size control; alpha remains 8-bit. Dimensions, RGBA format and exterior transparency were checked. Cutouts were reviewed over light, dark and magenta backgrounds, preserving identified white artwork areas.
manifest.json lists final PNG sizes and SHA-256 hashes. transparency-checks.json records internal background removal checks.
Inspect fine details and your product mockup at the intended print size. HEX colors are screen references, not guaranteed fabric shades: choose the closest available garment color.
