TECHNICAL NOTES
All final PNGs: 7000 x 7000 pixels, 8-bit RGBA, actual exterior transparency and transparency in identified internal background gaps.
Warm ivory fur, snow, whipped cream, icing, feathers and decorative highlights are intentionally part of the artwork, not background.
The navy drawn frames in images 01 and 08 are intentional graphic elements. Empty space within them is transparent; no paper card or paper drop shadow remains.
The source images were generated at 1024 x 1024 and enlarged with Lanczos interpolation. This is not native 7000px detail or AI super-resolution.
RGB colors use 32 levels per channel for file-size control; alpha remains 8-bit.
Dimensions, RGBA format and exterior transparency were verified. Source cutouts were reviewed on light, dark and magenta backgrounds.
manifest.json lists PNG sizes and SHA-256 hashes; transparency-checks.json records interior background removal checks.
