# Agent Memes Vol.16 — 10 unofficial fan art designs

## التحميل وفك الضغط على الكمبيوتر
1. حمّل ملف Agent_Memes_Vol16_10_Designs_7000x7000.zip من رابط التحميل المباشر وافتح مجلد Downloads.
2. اضغط كليك يمين على ملف ZIP واختار Extract All / استخراج الكل، وبعدها Extract. على Mac اضغط عليه مرتين.
3. افتح مجلد PNG_7000x7000 للصور العشرة. رقم الصورة هو نفس رقم بيانات العنوان والوصف والتاجات.
4. افتح Titles_And_Colors_EN_DE_FR_ES.txt للعناوين والألوان مجمعة، وListing_Copy_EN_DE_FR_ES.txt للوصف والتاجات باللغات الأربع. الملفات النصية UTF-8، والـCSV فيه BOM لتسهيل فتحه في Excel.

لو نافذة عارض الصور بتظهر لون أبيض أو أسود وراء الرسم، ده مش بالضرورة خلفية داخل الصورة؛ جرّبها فوق لون مختلف. preview.jpg بيعرض الألوان المقترحة فقط، ومش ملف طباعة.

## Contents and technical facts
- Exactly ten final PNG images, 7000 × 7000 pixels, RGBA, transparent exterior and selected enclosed background gaps. Complete characters and props are retained.
- Native AI-generated sources: 1408 × 768 pixels. Jett was isolated from the center variant of its native image (crop box: 480, 0, 930, 768). Artwork was trimmed, proportionally fitted to a 1024 × 1024 transparent working canvas (maximum artwork dimension 950 px), then enlarged using Lanczos to 7000 × 7000. This is an enlargement, not native 7000 px detail or a vector rendering. No stretching.
- PNG uses 8-bit RGBA storage. RGB is reduced to 32 levels per channel to keep downloads manageable; alpha retains 256 levels for edges. Inspect the artwork at your intended print size before publishing, especially small lines and facial details.
- White hair, eyes, shirt fabric, the lint roller, lens reflections, clock face and bubble-wrap highlights are intentional artwork, not unremoved background. The plant, orb, stool, loose leg, Wingman and small decorative marks are retained. The outer glow of Sage’s orb was de-matted to translucent teal rather than leaving an opaque white halo.
- Ten new humorous compositions, not reproductions of official game sprays. The clean PNG artwork contains no overlaid listing title or shirt-color backdrop.
- Each design has English, German, French and Spanish listing drafts, each with a 4–8-word title, one main tag and 14 unique supporting tags shorter than 50 characters each. EVERY main and supporting tag is one or two words in ALL FOUR languages, validated across all 600 tags (even hyphen-separated words are counted separately). See tag-word-validation.json. These are writing suggestions, not proof of search volume, sales or platform acceptance.
- Garment HEX values are screen references. Actual garment availability, dye and printing may differ; choose the closest available color and check the product preview. No claim of exact textile matching is made.
- manifest.json records final filenames, native dimensions, bytes and SHA-256. transparency-checks.json documents targeted gap cleanup. Source provenance and editable working PNG references are retained in the repository under source-reference.json and sources/ (not repeated in the customer ZIP).
