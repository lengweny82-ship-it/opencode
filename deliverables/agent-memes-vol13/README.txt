TECHNICAL NOTES
Each PNG is 7000 x 7000 pixels, 8-bit RGBA, with real exterior transparency and identified enclosed background gaps removed. Pale clothes, eyes, teeth, milk, the sock, gum highlights and cushion highlights are intentional artwork, not background. Magical forms and bubblegum are stylized colored illustrations, not optically transparent 3D materials.
Sources: 1408 x 768 for all ten images. For Iso and Cypher, a single complete figure/scene was selected from surplus generated duplicate fragments; those fragments are not part of the final artwork. No character was stretched or cut off at the final canvas edges.
Art was cropped and fitted proportionally, then enlarged using Lanczos interpolation. Not native 7000px detail or AI super-resolution. RGB uses 32 levels per channel for file-size control, alpha remains 8-bit. Inspect at the intended print size and check product mockups before publishing.
manifest.json contains source dimensions, PNG sizes and SHA-256 hashes. transparency-checks.json records the reviewed internal background components. Cutouts were reviewed on light, dark and magenta backgrounds. HEX codes are screen references, not guaranteed fabric shades: choose the closest available garment color.
All 40 titles have 4–8 words. Each localized entry has one main tag and 14 unique supporting tags, each under 50 characters. Natural wording is not a claim that these illustrations were drawn by hand.
