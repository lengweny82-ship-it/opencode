TECHNICAL NOTES
Every final PNG is 7000 x 7000 pixels, 8-bit RGBA, with actual exterior transparency and transparency in identified internal background gaps.
Ivory armor, equipment cases, painted highlights and colored interface panels are intentionally artwork, not background. The colored energy shields and holograms are stylized illustrated forms, not optically transparent 3D materials.
Source images were generated at 1024 x 1024, cropped and fitted without stretching, then enlarged using Lanczos interpolation. This is not native 7000px detail or AI super-resolution.
RGB colors use 32 levels per channel for file-size control; alpha remains 8-bit.
Dimensions, RGBA format and exterior transparency were checked. Source cutouts were reviewed over light, dark and magenta backgrounds.
manifest.json lists PNG sizes and SHA-256 hashes. transparency-checks.json records interior background removal checks.
Inspect the fine details and product mockup at your intended print size before publishing. HEX colors are screen references, not guaranteed fabric shades: choose the closest available garment color.
