## Contents and technical facts
- Partial delivery: exactly NINE finished duo compositions, 01–09. The requested tenth scene (Astra + Harbor decorating a cupcake) failed to generate and is not included. No tenth PNG or listing is claimed. generation-status.json records the missing item.
- Final images: 7000 × 7000 pixels, RGBA PNG, transparent exterior and selected enclosed background gaps. Complete characters and props are retained.
- Native AI-generated source sizes: eight images at 1408 × 768; Sage + Hush at 768 × 1365. See manifest.json for each image. Sources were trimmed, proportionally fitted to a transparent 1024 × 1024 working canvas (artwork maximum 950 px), then enlarged to 7000 × 7000 using Lanczos. This is an enlargement, not native 7000 px detail or vector rendering. No stretching.
- PNG uses 8-bit RGBA storage. RGB is reduced to 32 levels per channel to keep downloads manageable; alpha retains 256 levels. Inspect at intended print size before uploading, especially small facial details and lines.
- Intended whites in hair, eyes, clothes, cream dishes and reflections are retained. Detached yarn, dirt crumbs and smoke are part of the drawing, not dirt in the cutout. The cyan searchlight is an intentional partly transparent effect; white-matte contamination was reduced in its open section. Neutral exterior shadows were removed.
- Original humorous fan compositions, not reproductions of official game sprays. No overlaid listing title or garment-color backdrop appears inside the print PNGs.
- 36 listing drafts: nine designs in English, German, French and Spanish. Every title has 4–8 words. Every language/design has one main tag and 14 unique supporting tags shorter than 50 characters each. ALL 540 tags are one or two words; even hyphen-separated words are counted separately. See tag-word-validation.json. This is descriptive writing, not proof of search traffic, sales or marketplace approval.
- Garment HEX values are screen references only. Choose the closest available garment and check the product preview. Dye, printing and available product colors can differ.
- Final filenames, native dimensions, bytes and SHA-256 are in manifest.json; RGBA, dimensions and alpha checks are in export-validation.json; targeted gap cleanup is in transparency-checks.json.
- Native source and transparent working PNG references are preserved in the repository under sources/ and source-reference.json, not repeated inside the customer ZIP.
