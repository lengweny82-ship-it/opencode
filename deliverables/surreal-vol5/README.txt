TECHNICAL NOTES
Each final PNG is 7000 x 7000 pixels, 8-bit RGBA, with actual exterior transparency.
Source artwork was generated at 1024 x 1024 and resized using Lanczos interpolation. It is not native 7000px detail or AI super-resolution.
RGB colors use 32 levels per channel for file-size control; alpha remains 8-bit.
Enclosed light details in the artwork are not necessarily transparent.
Inspect fine details, edges and product mockups at your intended print size.
The illustrated background colors in the preview are approximate screen colors, not guaranteed fabric colors. Pick the closest available garment color on the marketplace.
